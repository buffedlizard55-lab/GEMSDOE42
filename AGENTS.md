# Repository work instructions

Before any project work, read `README.md` from top to bottom. It is the permanent charter and evidence ledger. Then read the relevant research page(s) and check `docs/irregularities.md` before using external claims or prior scores.

Non-negotiables:
- Never bypass DrivenData authentication, automate access to its pages, use/stash credentials, or submit to the competition on a participant's behalf.
- Never create a purported competition TIFF from synthetic, guessed, misaligned, or unverified inputs. Keep the download disabled until the exact template, output checks, holdout gate, and prior-raster correlation gate pass.
- Do not copy or modify an earlier competition submission as a prediction. Compare prior raw TIFFs only for a documented distinctness audit.
- Do not spend a weekly submission slot until the candidate beats the frozen spatially blocked holdout incumbent under the preregistered protocol.
- Separate official facts, local measurements, user/owner claims, hypotheses, and unknowns. Attach source links and observation dates; flag contradictions instead of resolving them by guess.
- After implementation, run tests, inspect `git diff --check`, and perform multiple review passes against the original charter.

The authoritative external submission format and rules are linked in `README.md`. The official public leaderboard/competition pages must not be polled by automation; DrivenData's Terms of Use prohibit automated monitoring absent prior written consent.
