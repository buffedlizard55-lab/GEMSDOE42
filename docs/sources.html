Data provenance rules

  • Keep original source, URL/DOI, license, retrieval time, checksum, CRS, transform, resolution, shape, band description and nodata semantics with each data object.
  • Do not call a layer “officially aligned” until its metadata and pixels have been checked against the competition sample grid.
  • Keep competition data and generated rasters outside Git. Do not mirror access-controlled contest files or redistribute third-party data without checking license terms.
  • Every future leaderboard snapshot must state its access timestamp, and must be retrieved in a way consistent with the platform Terms of Use.
