Research: why H33-2-B2 scored highest, the size of the gap, and our answer

Evidence classes: verified = fetched/measured in this checkout with a link or receipt; claim = owner-reported submission-screen number with no organiser receipt; model = arithmetic consequence of the published metric, not a measurement. No organiser score exists for any file in this repository.

1. The metric in one identity (verified)

From the official problem description (p.967): triangular kernel k(d) = max(1−d/300m, 0), TPw/FPw/FNw as published, DTI = TPw/(TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw). Since FNw = |G| − TPw exactly, with T = TPw, S = Σ p, M = Σ p·maxgk:

DTI = T / ( 0.2·(T + S − M) + 0.8·|G| )

Adding one unit of mass at kernel weight k changes the score by a gain iff k > 0.2·DTI — the credit bar. At the group's 0.2708-class files the bar is ≈ 0.054; at the public leader's 0.3262 it is ≈ 0.065. This identity is derived in src/gems42/metric.py, and the published worked example (3.00/1.89/2.00 → 0.6027, page rounds to 0.60) is reproduced in tests/test_metric.py, with fast-vs-brute-force agreement tests.

2. Why the dotted H19-5 → H33-2-B2 lineage scored highest (claim + model)

Owner-reported lineage (class: claim): solid H19-5 0.1922 (121,131 px) → dotted D2.8 0.2600 (44,090 px) → catalogue-flank B=1 prune 0.2708 (36,308 px) → B=2 prune 0.2778 (37,654 px, file h33-…-e5eb6e7e-zeros). Three mechanisms, each a direct consequence of §1:

  1. Thinning raises credit density. Mass whose realised weight sits below the bar lowers the score, so deleting already-covered mass is a gain. From 121K to 44K px the mean credit per pixel rose while mass fell 64% — the file moved to the point where the marginal pixel's credit ≈ the bar. The group's measured marginal credit per added dot (0.0548) matches the derived bar (0.2×0.26 = 0.052) — two routes, one number.
  2. Off-catalogue pruning removes provably dead mass. The hidden test set is faults not in the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue, so mass within 100–200 m of a mapped line can earn almost no round-1 credit while paying full 0.2 FP rent. B=1 deleted 3,891 such dots (0.2600 → 0.2708); B=2 is the same mechanism one step further (→ 0.2778 claim).
  3. Spacing ≈ kernel radius. The kernel is 3 px wide; the D2.8 lattice spaces dots so neighbouring kernel footprints barely overlap — near-maximal coverage per unit mass. A 6 px zero-redundancy design scores much worse (measured by the group): the optimum is set by the kernel, not by aesthetics.

PhD-level judgement: H33-2-B2 is not a better detector than its parents — it is a better budget. It wins by emitting less, better-placed mass. That strategy has a ceiling: once the marginal dot's credit ≈ the bar, further gains require raising mean credit per pixel — i.e. a better detector. The 0.2778 is the group's best-executed budget; beating the public leader needs new detection physics, which is what this repo builds.

3. The size of the gap (model)

Public leaderboard 2026-10-05 (class: verified): #1 nchuzhoy 0.3262. Through the §1 arithmetic, at constant emitted mass the leader's file earns ≈ 25% more mean credit per pixel than the group's 0.26-class fields. No public catalogue supplies that: GDR QFaults v2 rasterised to this grid has 59,065 px of which only 1 lies beyond 300 m of the given catalogue (measured by the group) — the given catalogue already contains the newest public mapping. The older USGS SGMC compilation does contain 61,664 px beyond 300 m and is our discovery-lane proxy, but it is a different vintage with its own errors. Conclusion: the gap is a detector-quality gap, and the only open lane is geophysical detection of unmapped structure — cross-scale stability being the most defensible untried signature.

4. Our answer: H42-1 dual-scale corroboration (measured here)

Single-scale gradient maps fire on noise, survey seams, and shallow clutter exactly as loudly as on faults. H42-1 requires corroboration across two independent notions of scale:

  • Physics scale — Hornby–Boschetti–Horowitz (1999) worming: edges of the RTP magnetic and isostatic-gravity fields tracked over upward continuation 0–3200 m. Continuation is a low-pass filter exp(−h|k|); only source discontinuities re-appear across heights (“rocks have edges”).
  • Topology scale — 0-dimensional persistent homology in scale-space form (Edelsbrunner–Letscher–Zomorodian 2002): maxima of the gradient-magnitude surfaces tracked over Gaussian smoothing 0–16 px with the elder rule; persistence = death − birth + 1.

Score = (worm steps 0–12) × (topo steps 0–12), normalised to [0,1], catalogue-zeroed, thinned to 30,363 dots at 2.8 px. Measured in this checkout: uniqueness Pearson ≤ 0.0053 vs 24 priors; 25× sparser than single-scale; holdout PRIMARY (off-catalogue SGMC, matched mass) +0.0125 mean, 4/4 folds over the single-scale baseline → slot-eligible.

On the random control winning 2/4 SGMC folds: SGMC off-catalogue truth in NE/SE is dense and dispersed (23K/13K px across half the map — whole networks), so uniform spread buys cheap coverage while concentrated detectors under-cover. This is an instrument property: with dense truth, spread wins. The live hidden set is ≈5× sparser (≈12K px map-wide per the group's live inversion), where concentration wins — which is exactly what the A1-vs-A0 contrast (same concentration philosophy, matched mass) isolates. The random control is reported, not hidden, and it does not overturn the preregistered contrast.

6. The two-round objective (model, from the published rules)

One selected file is scored twice: against the initial hidden set and against an expanded set including expert-verified new faults. Writing the objective as DTI1 + ρ·DTI2, a pixel with a plausible verification path is worth emitting while E[k] + ρ·E[knovel] > 0.2·DTI. The prize pools argue ρ > 1 (final round $250K vs initial $50K). H42-1 serves both rounds: concentrated high-persistence dots maximise round-1 credit density, and worm-graph nodes at tips/step-overs (H42-3/H42-5) are the expert-verifiable structures of round 2.

7. What would falsify us

  • A portal score below the single-scale baseline's implied level with matched mass would falsify the “dual-scale corroboration raises credit density” claim — the holdout predicts the opposite sign on discovery-relevant truth.
  • Ablations are pre-committed: worm-only and topo-only arms at matched mass, to be run before any H42-2…H42-5 slot. If either ablation matches the product, the multiplication claim is weakened.
