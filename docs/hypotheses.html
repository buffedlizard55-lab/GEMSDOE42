H42-1 Worming × persistence (shipped). Faults as dual-scale-stable edges. RTP (band 2) + isostatic gravity (band 13) of training_features.tif; labels.tif only as a removal mask. Hornby–Boschetti–Horowitz worms at 0–3200 m continuation × scale-space maxima persistence (sigmas 0–16 px, elder rule); score = steps × steps. Zero labels in the detector; catalogue pixels hard-zeroed, so every dot is off-catalogue by construction; hidden test = faults not in the catalogue. First dual-scale-corroboration detector in the group: no prior arm multiplies continuation survival by topological persistence, on either layer. PRIMARY +0.0125, 4/4 folds over single-scale at matched mass · cost: built · data: in repo. SHIPPED (UNSCIRED)
H42-2 Tilt-angle worms on TMI. The tilt angle normalises anomaly amplitude, so weak edges of shallow buried faults (the hidden population) get equal vote with strong ones. TMI (band 14) + TMI vertical gradient (band 9); RTP as corroboration. Tilt = atan(VG / |HG|); zero-contour + worm survival of the tilt surface across continuation. Targets the location of contacts independent of magnetisation strength (Salem et al. 2007). Buried/weakly-magnetic faults have no surface trace (unmapped) but still deflect the tilt zero-contour; amplitude-normalisation is exactly the “see what mappers missed” operator. H42-1 worms the raw fields; no arm has wormed a phase/normalised transform. Different failure mode, complementary misses. Expected small-positive on PRIMARY (weak-edge recall) · cost low (1 transform + existing ladder) · data in repo. QUEUED
H42-3 Worm-intersection nodes × MT conductance. Fault intersections/step-overs are the permeable nodes that host geothermal upflow; conductive clay caps (MT) mark the same nodes. H42-1 worm lines + MT conductance maps (GDR 1391, via doi:10.5066/P9TWT2LU) + 2 m probe corroboration. Graph nodes where ≥2 worm segments meet within 300 m, weighted by shallow (2–10 km) conductance; emission only at corroborated nodes. Catalogue traces are mapped as isolated lines; intersections of unmapped cross-structures are systematically missing, yet are the highest-permeability targets experts verify in round 2. No arm has built a worm graph or used MT conductance as a node weight. Pure addition arm on top of H42-1 geometry. Expected small on round 1, unbounded on round 2 (expert-verifiable nodes) · cost medium (graph + MT regrid) · data: VERIFIED OBTAINABLE, GDR 1391 links live (see sources). QUEUED
H42-4 2 m temperature-probe anomaly mask as corroborated addition. Shallow conductive heat marks permeable structure the 100 m geophysics cannot resolve. GDR 1391 “2m Temperature Probes” (1.03 MB, in-mirror) + H42-1 score as the corroboration filter + catalogue buffer. Probe-temperature anomaly surface (IDW, 100 m grid); emit only where anomaly ∩ (H42-1 score > 0) ∩ off-catalogue — a mask, not a surface, so mass stays inside the credit budget. Probe network samples ground truth heat, not mapped lines; GEMSDOE32 measured uncorroborated thermal addition at 0.002–0.005 credit/dot vs the 0.054 bar (falsified) — corroboration is the fix being tested. H42-1 uses no thermal data; H33-1 was falsified uncorroborated, H33-3 registered-but-unbuilt. This is the corroborated variant neither repo built. Expected ~0 on PRIMARY (sparse probes) unless corroboration concentrates it · cost low (zip already mirrored) · data in mirror. QUEUED
H42-5 Paleo-geothermal prior on worm tips/step-overs. Sinter/travertine/tufa mark where the crust already leaked fluid — present-day permeable structure. GDR 1391 “Paleo Geothermal Features” (82 kB, in-mirror) + H42-1 worm skeleton (tips + releasing bends). Tip/step-over extension of the worm skeleton, emitted only within 500 m of a paleo feature: a geological prior over where to extend, not a detector. Tips are by definition past the end of mapped traces (off-catalogue by construction); paleo features carry a fluid-flow attribute the fault catalogue lacks. No tip/step-over operator exists in this repo (H33-5 was falsified as an uncorroborated addition at 0.0077 credit/dot); the paleo gate is the new mechanism. Expected small-positive on PRIMARY · cost medium (skeleton + rasterise) · data in mirror. QUEUED

Ranking rationale (expected DTI improvement ÷ cost)

  1. H42-1 first: only arm with a measured PRIMARY pass (+0.0125, 4/4); cost already sunk; it is the base every other arm modifies.
  2. H42-2 second: lowest cost (one transform, same ladder/code), directly targets the buried-fault population, falsifiable in one holdout run.
  3. H42-3 third: highest round-2 upside (expert-verifiable permeable nodes) but medium cost and an external regrid step.
  4. H42-4 fourth: low cost but the uncorroborated variant already falsified nearby — the corroboration fix must prove itself from a low prior.
  5. H42-5 fifth: medium cost, narrow emission domain (tips near paleo features); small mass means small score movement either way.

Falsification record: nothing in this repo has been falsified yet except the single-scale baseline it beats. H33-1/H33-5 falsifications cited above are GEMSDOE32's measured results (owner checkout, labelled accordingly) and are used as priors, not as verification.
